# Fourier Series Square Wave — Manim Viral Shorts Storyboard

Target: 1080x1920 vertical Shorts, 30 fps, ~58s.
Topic: a square wave is the sum of odd sine harmonics — Fourier series.

## Segments

1. **hook** (≈3.0s)
   - Visual: title "A square wave from circles?" + a dashed square wave.
   - Audio: "What if I told you a square wave is made of spinning circles?"

2. **setup** (≈5.0s)
   - Visual: axes appear; a clean sine wave `sin(t)` is drawn.
   - Audio: "Any repeating shape can be built from sine waves."

3. **formula** (≈6.0s)
   - Visual: the Fourier series formula appears centered.
   - Audio: "A square wave equals four over pi, times the sum of odd sine harmonics."

4. **harmonic_1** (≈6.0s)
   - Visual: `4/π sin(t)` is drawn; this is the first approximation.
   - Audio: "Start with the first harmonic."

5. **harmonic_3** (≈7.0s)
   - Visual: `sin(3t)/3` term is added; the sum morphs closer to a square wave.
   - Audio: "Add the third harmonic, and the shape starts to sharpen."

6. **harmonic_5** (≈7.0s)
   - Visual: add `sin(5t)/5`; edges get steeper.
   - Audio: "The fifth harmonic adds more corners."

7. **harmonic_7** (≈7.0s)
   - Visual: add `sin(7t)/7`; the flat tops appear.
   - Audio: "Seven terms, and it already looks like a staircase."

8. **harmonic_9** (≈7.0s)
   - Visual: add `sin(9t)/9`; the wave is nearly square.
   - Audio: "Nine terms — the square wave is almost perfect."

9. **payoff** (≈6.0s)
   - Visual: dashed ideal square wave appears behind the sum.
   - Audio: "Keep adding odd harmonics forever, and you get a perfect square wave."

10. **formula_reveal** (≈5.0s)
    - Visual: full formula boxed and highlighted.
    - Audio: "This is the Fourier series."

11. **cta** (≈4.0s)
    - Visual: handle / follow text + the final graph in background.
    - Audio: "Follow for more math magic."
