# Reference Breakdown — Space Architecture Short

## 1. 3Blue1Brown — "Linear combinations, span, and basis vectors" (YouTube, 7.2M views)
- **Pattern**: slow reveal of basis vectors `i-hat`, `j-hat`; coordinates as scalars; parallelogram construction.
- **For us**: Use the same parallelogram reveal for `x i-hat + y j-hat`. Keep math labels large and centered.

## 2. 3Blue1Brown — "Change of basis" (YouTube, 2.5M views)
- **Pattern**: different basis grids overlay the same space; shearing animations.
- **For us**: Show alternative basis `u`, `w` reaching the same vector; use red color to distinguish.

## 3. Professor Dave Explains — "Basis and Dimension" (YouTube, 793K views)
- **Pattern**: direct-to-camera + whiteboard; defines basis as linearly independent spanning set.
- **For us**: Emphasize "số vector tối thiểu = số chiều" with a counting animation.

## 4. Clawdemy — "Spans and basis, in brief"
- **Pattern**: interactive 2D canvas, drag vectors to see span as a filled region.
- **For us**: Grid-of-dots reveal for span; keep plane translucent so axes stay visible.

## 5. Khan Academy — "Linear combinations and span"
- **Pattern**: simple coordinate-plane animations, step-by-step scaling and addition.
- **For us**: Use small-step animations: scale first, then translate, then add.

## 6. MIT OpenCourseWare — "Vector spaces" (Gilbert Strang)
- **Pattern**: rigorous but visual; treats vectors as independent of coordinate systems.
- **For us**: Keep the mantra "tọa độ chỉ là cách kéo giãn cơ sở".

## 7. Numberphile — "The Meaning of Dimension" (various guest explainers)
- **Pattern**: big idea in under 5 minutes, enthusiastic host, minimal formulas.
- **For us**: Hook with a question, answer with a visual chain: point → vector → basis → dimension.

## 8. Zach Star — "But what is the dimension of a vector space?" (Shorts style)
- **Pattern**: fast cuts, bold text, counter animations.
- **For us**: Counting `1 → 2 → 3` should be a full-screen counter with camera shift.

## 9. StatQuest with Josh Starmer — "Dimensionality Reduction" (Shorts snippets)
- **Pattern**: bouncy music, repeated phrase "It's just a vector!".
- **For us**: Repeat "i-hát / j-hát / k-hát" as building blocks; end with AI data as 100+ dimensions.

## 10. MinutePhysics — "Why is space 3D?" (Short)
- **Pattern**: playful 3D axes, arrows, and stick figures.
- **For us**: 3D camera swing around the `i, j, k` axes for depth.

## 11. 3Blue1Brown — "The essence of linear algebra" playlist
- **Pattern**: consistent visual vocabulary (yellow `i-hat`, green `j-hat`, moving vectors).
- **For us**: Keep consistent colors and re-use basis vectors in every scene.

## 12. Veritasium / TikTok math shorts — "Why does AI need so many dimensions?"
- **Pattern**: bar-chart feature vectors, high-dimensional spaces explained through data.
- **For us**: Show a 12-bar feature vector and a moving data dot to bridge math and AI.

## Synthesis
- Start with a hook question about where numbers live.
- Build the story: point → vector → coordinates as scalars of basis vectors → linear combination → span → basis → dimension → high-dimensional data.
- Use 3D camera, consistent basis-vector colors, and a dimension counter for retention.
