# Plot a Point in Space — Manim Shorts storyboard

Source inspiration: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/uupDWANsiRc
Topic: plotting a point in 3D Cartesian space, point (3, 2, 5).

## Segments

1. **title** (≈2.0s)
   - Visual: title card "Plot a Point in Space" + handle "@learningverse123" on black.
   - Audio: "Plot a point in space."

2. **axes** (≈5.0s)
   - Visual: 3D x, y, z axes fade in; floor grid appears; labels "x", "y", "z".
   - Camera slowly rotates.
   - Audio: "We need three axes: x, y, and z."

3. **coords** (≈4.0s)
   - Visual: text "(x, y, z)" appears fixed on screen.
   - Audio: "A point in 3D is written as x, y, z."

4. **plot_x** (≈6.0s)
   - Visual: a moving dot travels from the origin 3 units along the x-axis, leaving a trail; a dashed line drops from the moving dot to the x-axis with label "3".
   - Audio: "Start at the origin, and move 3 units along x."

5. **plot_y** (≈6.0s)
   - Visual: the dot moves from (3, 0, 0) parallel to y to (3, 2, 0); a dashed line to the y-axis labels "2"; the xy-grid rectangle is traced.
   - Audio: "Then 2 units parallel to y."

6. **plot_z** (≈6.0s)
   - Visual: the dot rises from (3, 2, 0) parallel to z to (3, 2, 5); a dashed line to the z-axis labels "5"; vertical edges of the box are drawn.
   - Audio: "Finally, 5 units up, parallel to z."

7. **reveal** (≈7.0s)
   - Visual: the final yellow point pulses; a translucent coordinate box appears; all three dashed projection lines are shown; the label "(3, 2, 5)" appears; camera orbits the point.
   - Audio: "So the point is (3, 2, 5)."

8. **outro** (≈4.5s)
   - Visual: title and handle reappear; axes and point stay in background.
   - Audio: "Plotting in 3D is just three steps. Subscribe for more math."
