# Quantum Computing Short — Storyboard

## Global rules
- Canvas: 1080 × 1920, 60 fps target, fallback 30 fps if needed.
- Background: near-black `#05070A` with a faint cyan/violet grid/nebula layer (very low opacity).
- Continuity motif: a single cyan-white quantum particle/photon travels from the opening binary tunnel through every scene and returns at the end.
- Typography: `Arial` for Vietnamese, `MathTex` for `|0\rangle`, `|1\rangle`, `|\psi\rangle`, `H`, `\alpha`, `\beta`.
- Safe area: 80 px sides, 120 px top, 300 px bottom reserved for Shorts UI.

---

## SCENE 1 — Cold open / myth bust (0.00–3.50 s)
### Voice
"Máy tính lượng tử không nhanh vì thử mọi đáp án cùng lúc."

### Visuals
- 0.00–0.30: Full-screen binary tunnel (0/1 digits) rushing toward camera. The continuity particle is a bright cyan streak in the center.
- 0.30–1.10: The digits fan out into many branching "answer" paths; text `THỬ MỌI ĐÁP ÁN?` materializes from the particles.
- 1.10–1.70: A red X slices across the text, branching paths shatter into particles.
- 1.70–3.50: From the debris, a glowing qubit sphere (simple sphere with two amplitude bands) forms; small text `KHÔNG HẲN.` appears and fades. Two faint wave bands emerge from the qubit.

### Camera
Fast push-in through tunnel, sudden stop at 1.10, slow dolly back as qubit forms.

### Sound
- 0.00: Low bass impact as binary data hits center.
- 1.10: Digital shatter + red X whoosh.
- 1.70: Soft quantum pulse as qubit appears.

## SCENE 2 — Classical bit (3.50–8.00 s)
### Voice
"Bí mật thật sự là: nó điều khiển sóng xác suất. Máy tính thường dùng bit, mỗi lúc chỉ là không hoặc một."

### Visuals
- 3.50–4.50: Two cyan waves from the qubit sweep across screen; text `SÓNG XÁC SUẤT` reveals along the wave crest.
- 4.50–6.00: Match cut / morph into a chunky 3D bit cube with a mechanical switch on top.
- 6.00–8.00: The switch snaps to `0` (bottom lights up), then to `1` (top lights up). A small timeline below shows a single dot moving between `0` and `1` only.

### Camera
Track the wave horizontally, then settle on the bit cube from a slight angle.

### Sound
- 3.50: Wave interference whoosh.
- 6.00/7.00: Snappy mechanical switch click.

## SCENE 3 — Qubit and Bloch sphere (8.00–17.00 s)
### Voice
"Qubit có trạng thái an-pha nhân ket không, cộng bê-ta nhân ket một. Trước khi đo, hai biên độ cùng tồn tại, nhưng không thể đọc như hai đáp án."

### Visuals
- 8.00–9.50: The bit cube morphs into a Bloch sphere with labeled axes `|0⟩` (north) and `|1⟩` (south). The continuity particle orbits and lands at the north pole.
- 9.50–11.50: Formula `|ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩` appears term by term. `α|0⟩` and `β|1⟩` pulse softly.
- 11.50–14.00: A state vector grows from the sphere center; its projection onto the `|0⟩`/`|1⟩` axes creates two amplitude bars. Formula `|α|² + |β|² = 1` fades in small.
- 14.00–17.00: Two ghostly copies of `|0⟩` and `|1⟩` appear as components, then a measurement aperture sweeps across and the copies merge back into a single state vector.

### Camera
Short 30° orbit around the Bloch sphere while state vector appears; pull back for projection view.

### Sound
- 9.50: Soft tonal hum for formula appearance.
- 14.00: Measurement sweep shutter + merge tone.

## SCENE 4 — Hadamard and measurement (17.00–25.00 s)
### Voice
"Cổng Hadamard xoay qubit trên mặt cầu Bloch. Khi đo, ta vẫn chỉ nhận không hoặc một."

### Visuals
- 17.00–18.50: A horizontal quantum wire extends from the Bloch sphere; a box labeled `H` slides in from the left.
- 18.50–21.00: The `H` gate activates; the state vector rotates from north pole to the equator. Formula morphs `|0⟩ → (|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√2`.
- 21.00–23.00: Two amplitude bars rise to equal 50 % each.
- 23.00–25.00: A measurement gate (aperture icon) closes on the wire, the 3D space collapses to a single classical bit. Text `ĐO → CHỈ 0 HOẶC 1` appears.

### Camera
Follow the gate as it slides, then follow the vector tip during rotation.

### Sound
- 18.50: Electric pulse for gate activation.
- 20.00: Tonal riser during rotation.
- 23.50: Camera shutter + digital snap.

## SCENE 5 — Two qubits / Bell state (25.00–35.00 s)
### Voice
"Với hai qubit, Hadamard rồi C-NOT tạo trạng thái Bell: chỉ không-không và một-một xuất hiện, với kết quả tương quan."

### Visuals
- 25.00–26.50: Camera pulls back to reveal two parallel quantum wires. Two qubit dots appear.
- 26.50–28.00: `H` gate on first wire splits the first qubit into two amplitude paths (faint diverging beams).
- 28.00–30.00: A `CNOT` gate appears: control dot on wire 1, target ⊕ on wire 2. A pulse travels from control to target.
- 30.00–33.00: Formula `(|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2` forms. `|00⟩` and `|11⟩` glow as two linked light structures; `|01⟩` and `|10⟩` appear dim then cancel (red X over them).
- 33.00–35.00: Two measurements run simultaneously. A tiny result strip shows `00`, `11`, `00`, `11` — no `01` or `10`.

### Camera
Pull-back reveal, then pan along the wires, slight zoom into the entangled pair.

### Sound
- 26.50: First gate pulse.
- 28.00: CNOT trigger pulse.
- 30.50: Low hit as wrong states cancel.
- 33.50: Two synchronized measurement snaps.

## SCENE 6 — Interference climax (35.00–47.00 s)
### Voice
"Sức mạnh nắm ở giao thoa. Thuật toán chỉnh pha để đường sai triệt tiêu, còn đường hữu ích cộng hưởng."

### Visuals
- 35.00–37.00: The Bell state dissolves into a field of amplitude paths (thin glowing lines).
- 37.00–40.00: Wrong paths are colored red/violet, shifted by `π` phase. They overlap and cancel; their amplitude bars shrink toward zero.
- 40.00–43.00: Useful paths (cyan) rotate phase until in-phase; their waves stack and amplitude bars double.
- 43.00–45.00: All cyan waves lock into a tall central amplitude column. Text `SAI → TRIỆT TIÊU` then transforms to `ĐÚNG → CỘNG HƯỞNG`.
- 45.00–47.00: Camera rushes forward through the resonant column.

### Camera
Fly into the path field, push through the interference region, dolly into the final column.

### Sound
- 37.00: Phase-shift warble.
- 40.00: Low rumble rise.
- 43.00: Main interference hit / riser peak.
- 45.00: Beat drop or low-frequency hit.

## SCENE 7 — Measurement and output (47.00–54.00 s)
### Voice
"Phép đo biến mẫu xác suất thành kết quả cổ điển."

### Visuals
- 47.00–49.50: The wave field morphs into a probability histogram. One column is tall (useful answer), wrong columns are much shorter.
- 49.50–52.00: A measurement sweep moves left to right; all bars collapse into a single classical bit.
- 52.00–54.00: The result bit flies into a classical computer screen. Text `QUANTUM PROCESS → CLASSICAL RESULT` types in.

### Camera
Pull back from the wave field to reveal the histogram, then follow the collapsing bit.

### Sound
- 47.00: Wave-to-histogram morph shimmer.
- 49.50: Measurement sweep whoosh.
- 52.00: Bit landing click.

## SCENE 8 — Conclusion / loop (54.00–59.30 s)
### Voice
"Máy tính lượng tử không thay laptop. Nó là cỗ máy chuyên dụng để thiết kế giao thoa. Đó mới là điều kỳ lạ."

### Visuals
- 54.00–56.00: Camera pulls out from the classical screen to reveal the whole quantum circuit inside a stylized dilution refrigerator / quantum chip.
- 56.00–58.00: Large text `KHÔNG PHẢI NHIỀU ĐÁP ÁN.` transforms to `LÀ GIAO THOA ĐƯỢC THIẾT KẾ.`
- 58.00–59.30: The quantum chip dissolves into the same cyan particle from Scene 1, which streaks back toward a binary tunnel to create a loop. Small CTA: `Phần sau: Thuật toán Grover?`

### Camera
Smooth pull-out, then fast transition back to the opening tunnel perspective.

### Sound
- 56.00: Clean text transition click.
- 58.00: Particle streak / loop riser.
