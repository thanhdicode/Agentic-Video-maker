# What if Gravity Disappeared?

## Scene 1: Hook
What if gravity suddenly disappeared? Let's find out!

## Scene 2: Floating Everything
Firstly, everything not tied down would start floating away into space.

## Scene 3: Atmosphere Gone
Secondly, Earth's atmosphere would drift away, so we couldn't breathe.

## Scene 4: Oceans Float Away
Thirdly, oceans, rivers, and lakes would form giant floating blobs.

## Scene 5: Moon Runs Away
The Moon would no longer orbit Earth and would fly off into deep space.

## Scene 6: Planets Go Crazy
Planets would stop orbiting the Sun and wander around in random directions.

## Scene 7: Weak Bodies
Without gravity, our muscles and bones would become weaker over time.

## Scene 8: Weird Fire
Flames would become round balls instead of pointing upward.

## Scene 9: Hard to Build
Building houses or writing would be really tricky because everything floats.

## Scene 10: Outro
But don't worry, gravity is here to stay, keeping us safe on Earth!
