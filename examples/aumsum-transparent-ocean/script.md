# What if Oceans were Transparent?

## Scene 1: Hook
What if our oceans were completely transparent?

## Scene 2: Fact 1
Firstly, submarines would lose one of their biggest advantages: stealth.

## Scene 3: Fact 2
Secondly, sea animals that use camouflage would suddenly become super easy to spot.

## Scene 4: Fact 3
Thirdly, underwater archaeology would become easier, but coral reefs might suffer from too much sunlight.

## Scene 5: Outro
But don't worry, oceans are beautiful just the way they are!
